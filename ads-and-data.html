Ads & Data in Unpredictable

Here is exactly what the game collects, in one table. It matches the Data safety section of our Google Play listing.

Unpredictable is free, shows ads, offers optional seed packs, and has no accounts. Two outside services receive data: the ad network, Google AdMob (Google LLC), and the in-app purchase system, Google Play Billing through Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity Technologies). Your game progress stays on your device. For the full details, see our Privacy Policy.

Data sent by the ad and purchase SDKs is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Your choices

  • Delete your advertising ID in Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Opt out of ads personalization in the same place (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Google ad settings: manage them at adssettings.google.com.
  • Consent prompt: in the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions where it's required, you'll see a consent prompt (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized. You can change your choice later in the game's Settings.
  • Delete your data: see Delete my data.
  • Read the details: see the Privacy Policy.
