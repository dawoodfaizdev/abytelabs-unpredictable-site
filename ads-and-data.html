Ads & Data in Unpredictable

Here is exactly what the game collects, in one table. It matches the Data safety section of our Google Play listing.

Unpredictable is free and shows ads, and has no accounts. The only outside service that receives data is the ad network, [AD NETWORK]. Your game progress stays on your device. For the full details, see our Privacy Policy.

Data sent by the ad SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Your choices

  • Reset your advertising ID in Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Opt out of personalized ads in the same place (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Consent prompt: in the EEA and UK we ask for your consent before personalized ads.
  • Delete your data: see Delete my data.
  • Read the details: see the Privacy Policy.
