Quick answers

How do I jump higher?

Hold your finger down. A quick tap is a small hop, and the longer you keep your finger down, the higher Sprig jumps.

How do I duck?

Swipe down quickly and keep your finger down. Sprig stays low for as long as you hold.

Why do I see ads?

Unpredictable is free to play, and ads are how we pay for it. You can read exactly what the ads collect on our Ads & Data page.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, but only on the device you play on. There are no accounts, so your progress doesn't move to a new phone. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game resets it.

How do I replay the tutorial?

Open Settings in the game and replay it from there.

How do I report a bug?

Email us at support.abytelabs@gmail.com with your phone model, your Android version and a short description of what happened. The "Report a bug" button above fills in a template for you.
