How to play

Three moves. That's the whole game, and one thumb is plenty.

Tap to hop

A quick tap makes Sprig hop over small bumps and puddles.

Hold for a high jump

Keep your thumb down a little longer to leap over the big stuff.

Swipe down to duck

Slide your thumb down to squat low and let trouble fly right over.

Meet Sprig

Sprig is a small sprout-headed kid with a big red scarf and an even bigger sense of adventure. The road never goes to plan, but Sprig keeps hopping, ducking and smiling anyway.

Why you'll like it

One thumb

Play on the bus, on the sofa, or with a snack in your other hand.

Short runs

Every run is over in a minute or two, so there's always time for one more.

Cozy art

Soft colours, round shapes and a road that feels like a warm hug.

Fair surprises

Hazards always give you a clear warning first. Unexpected, never unfair.
