Unpredictable

A cozy one-thumb runner where nothing is quite what you expect. Eight friends, three roads, and one very busy thumb.

  • 8 friends to play
  • 3 roads
  • A wardrobe to dress up
Get it on Google Play Coming soon

Now in closed testing on Google Play.

How to play

Three moves. That's the whole game, and one thumb is plenty.

Tap to hop

A quick tap makes Sprig hop over small bumps and puddles.

Hold for a high jump

Keep your thumb down a little longer to leap over the big stuff.

Swipe down to duck

Slide your thumb down to squat low and let trouble fly right over.

Three roads to run

Each road has its own sky, weather, music and surprises, and its own best distance to beat. Go far on one to open the next.

  • Sunny Lane

    Easy

    Sunny dunes into a summer storm.

    Open from the start

  • Misty Woods

    Medium

    Dawn mist, autumn leaves and fireflies.

    Opens at 300 m on Sunny Lane

  • Frost Peaks

    Hard

    Snowy and fast under the aurora.

    Opens at 300 m on Misty Woods

Seeds, and how to get them

Seeds unlock friends and outfits, and can buy you a second chance when a run goes wrong. Everything in the game can be earned just by playing.

Collect them on the road

Every seed you pick up on a run is yours to keep. Grab a few in a row for a little celebration.

Watch a short ad

Choose to watch an ad in the store for 25 seeds, up to ten times a day. Always optional.

Seed packs

Optional packs of seeds, from a handful to a treasure chest, bought through Google Play.

Out of luck mid-run? One button brings you back: with seeds when you have enough, or with a short ad when you don't.

Why you'll like it

One thumb

Play on the bus, on the sofa, or with a snack in your other hand.

Short runs

Every run is over in a minute or two, so there's always time for one more.

Cozy art

Soft colours, round shapes and a road that feels like a warm hug.

Fair surprises

Hazards always give you a clear warning first. Unexpected, never unfair.
