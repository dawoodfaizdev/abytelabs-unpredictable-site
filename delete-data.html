Delete your data

Five simple steps. Most of it takes under a minute.

  1. We have nothing to delete

    Unpredictable has no accounts, and we don't store personal data on our servers. So there's nothing for us to delete.

  2. Remove the data on your device

    Your progress and settings live only on your phone. To remove them, either:

    • open Android Settings > Apps > Unpredictable > Storage > Clear data, or
    • uninstall the game.

  3. Stop ad personalization or reset your advertising ID

    Open Android Settings > Privacy > Ads and choose Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization. (The exact wording can vary a little between phones.)

  4. Use the ad network's privacy controls

    The ads in the game come from [AD NETWORK], which handles its own data. Its controls are here: [AD NETWORK] privacy controls.

    Email us at [EMAIL]. We reply within [30] days.
