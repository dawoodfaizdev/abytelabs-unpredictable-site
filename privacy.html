This policy explains, in plain language, what happens with your information when you play Unpredictable, a game for Android made by Abyte Labs. The short version: there are no accounts, your progress stays on your phone, and the only outside service that handles data is the one that shows ads.

No account, no sign-in

Unpredictable doesn't need an account or a sign-in. We don't ask for your name, your email address or your location.

What stays on your device

Your game progress is saved only on your device. That includes:

  • your best distance,
  • your settings,
  • whether you've finished the tutorial, and
  • the seeds you've collected in the game.

We don't receive this information.

Ads

Unpredictable is free to play and shows ads. The ads are provided by [AD NETWORK, e.g. Google AdMob]. To show and measure ads, the ad network may collect and use:

  • your advertising ID,
  • your IP address,
  • information about your device and the app, and
  • your approximate location (worked out from your IP address).

It may also use cookies or similar technology. The ad network handles this information under its own privacy policy: [AD NETWORK] privacy policy.

Consent and your ad choices

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), we show a consent prompt before any personalized ads. You can change your choice later in Settings [IF IMPLEMENTED].

On any Android phone you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads. Look in Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact wording can vary a little between phones).

Children

Unpredictable is not directed to children under [13, or 16 where applicable], and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has given us personal information, please email [EMAIL] and we'll look into it.

Sharing and retention

We don't sell personal data. Data goes only to the services listed on this page: the ad network above and, if listed, the crash reporting service. Each of them keeps data under its own policy.

Deleting your data

  • On your device: uninstalling the game removes the progress and settings saved on your device.
  • Ad network data: reset your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Step-by-step instructions are on our Delete my data page.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal information a company holds about you,
  • ask for it to be corrected or deleted,
  • object to certain uses of it, or ask for it to be restricted,
  • take back consent you gave earlier, and
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

We don't ask for your name, email or location, so we usually have little or nothing to look up. Most ad-related data sits with the ad network, which has its own tools and policy (linked above). If you'd like to use any of these rights, or you're not sure how, email us at [EMAIL].
