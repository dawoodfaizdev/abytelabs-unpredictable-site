Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains, in plain language, what happens with your information when you play Unpredictable, a game for Android made by Abyte Labs. The short version: there are no accounts, your progress stays on your phone, and the only outside service that handles data is Google AdMob, which shows the ads.

No account, no sign-in

Unpredictable doesn't need an account or a sign-in. We don't ask for your name, your email address or your location, and we don't keep any personal data on our servers.

What stays on your device

Your game progress is stored only on your device. That includes:

  • your best distance,
  • your settings,
  • whether you've finished the tutorial, and
  • the seeds you've collected in the game.

We don't receive this information.

Ads

Unpredictable is free to play and shows ads. The ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect and use:

  • your advertising ID,
  • your IP address (which gives your approximate location),
  • information about your device and the app, and
  • your interactions with ads.

Google AdMob may also show personalized ads where this is allowed. Google handles this information under its own policies: see Google's Privacy & Terms and How Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services.

We don't use any analytics or crash-reporting services.

Consent and your ad choices

If you play in the European Economic Area, the UK or another region where it's required, you'll see a consent prompt (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized. You can change your choice later in the game's Settings.

On your Android phone you can also delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization. Look in Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact wording can vary a little between phones). You can manage your Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

Children

Unpredictable is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email support.abytelabs@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Sharing and retention

We don't sell personal data. Data goes only to Google AdMob, as described above, and Google keeps it under its own policies. We don't keep any personal data on our servers.

Deleting your data

  • On your device: uninstalling the game removes the progress and settings saved on your device.
  • Ad data: delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads), and manage your Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

Step-by-step instructions are on our Delete my data page.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal information a company holds about you,
  • ask for it to be corrected or deleted,
  • object to certain uses of it, or ask for it to be restricted,
  • take back consent you gave earlier, and
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

We don't ask for your name, email or location, so we usually have little or nothing to look up. Most ad-related data sits with Google, which has its own tools and policies (linked above). If you'd like to use any of these rights, or you're not sure how, email us at support.abytelabs@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date at the top.

Contact us

Questions about privacy? Email Abyte Labs at support.abytelabs@gmail.com.
